Privacy Policy & Children's Data Protection

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview & Commitment to UK GDPR

StoryMagic Hub is fully committed to protecting the privacy and security of children and parents. We strictly adhere to the UK GDPR and the ICO Children's Code.

2. How We Handle Children's Photos

Parents may optionally upload a photograph of their child to generate a personalized book illustration:

  • Temporary Processing: Photos are sent directly to secure AI vision models solely for illustration generation.
  • Immediate Deletion: Photos are never stored permanently on our servers and are deleted immediately after generation.
  • No Model Training: Uploaded photographs are never used to train public AI models.

3. Parental Consent

Book generation requires explicit confirmation that the user is the parent or legal guardian with authority to consent to the processing of the child's likeness.

4. Data Security

All data transmissions are encrypted using industry-standard protocols. Payment data is securely processed via Stripe.
