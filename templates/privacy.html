Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

SuperMeStory creates personalised illustrated stories for children. This policy explains what personal data we use, why, who helps us, how long we keep it, and what rights you have under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

1. Who we are

The data controller is Anatolijs Osipovs 114 Bishopsfield, Harlow, CM18 6UW, trading as SuperMeStory (supermestory.com). You can contact us about your data at hello@supermestory.com.

2. Who this service is for

SuperMeStory is for parents, legal guardians and adults authorised by them. You must be 18 or over to use it. We do not collect data directly from children: everything about a child is provided by the adult who creates the book.

4. Our legal bases

  • Contract: to create your book, deliver your purchase and run your account.
  • Consent: to process the child’s photo for illustrations. You give this when you tick the consent box before generating a book, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting the book or your account.
  • Legal obligation: to keep accounting and tax records.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure and prevent misuse (for example the daily limit on free previews).

6. How long we keep data

  • Child’s photo: not stored by us.
  • Unpaid previews: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Paid books: kept in your account until you delete them or your account.
  • Delivery details: removed as soon as the print order has been accepted by our printer. If an order cannot be sent, they are removed after 30 days at most.
  • Payment and order records: kept for up to 6 years for accounting and tax purposes, as the law requires.
  • Our printer and payment provider keep their own records of orders and payments under their own policies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time.

  • Delete a book: use the bin button on the book in My Books. It is removed immediately.
  • Delete your account and all your books: use Delete my account and all my books at the bottom of My Books.
  • Anything else (a copy of your data, a correction, a question): email hello@supermestory.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

8. Cookies

We use one cookie, sms_session, which keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the service, so it does not need your consent. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

9. Security

Connections to our website are encrypted, passwords are stored only as hashes, and each account can only see its own books. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you on the website.
